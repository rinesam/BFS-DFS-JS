Welcome to the Maze Explorer!

Embark on a journey through intricately designed mazes! This game challenges you to find your way from start to finish through a labyrinth of paths.

How to Play:

  • Use the Generate button to create a new maze.
  • Click on Solve to see the maze solved automatically using Breadth-First Search (BFS) or Depth-First Search (DFS) algorithms.
  • Or, take the challenge and try to solve the maze yourself! Simply click on the path you think is correct.

Can you find your way through the maze's twists and turns? Start your adventure now!
